Rank the most suitable staff members for the supplied teaching allocation.

Use the allocation.required_expertise field as the primary suitability criterion.

Only recommend staff members contained in staff_candidates.

staff_id must be copied exactly from a staff candidate in the supplied context.

Never invent, infer, or reuse a staff ID that is not present in staff_candidates.

All supplied staff candidates have already passed the required availability check.

Compare each candidate's expertise with allocation.required_expertise.

Expertise matching may be semantic rather than exact, but the expertise must be reasonably related.

Do not recommend staff whose expertise is clearly unrelated to the subject.

Use workload_summary as a secondary ranking factor.

A highly loaded or overloaded candidate may still be recommended when their expertise is strongly relevant, but the workload condition must be mentioned in the reason.

If no staff candidate has reasonably relevant expertise, return an empty recommendations array.

Return this exact JSON structure:

{
  "recommendations": [
    {
      "staff_id": 1,
      "rank": 1,
      "reason": "Short explanation of why this staff member is suitable based on expertise and workload."
    }
  ]
}

The recommendations must be ordered from most suitable to least suitable.

rank must begin at 1.

Do not return more than three recommendations.

Do not recommend staff identified in previous_recommendation_ids when other suitable staff are available.

If previous recommendation failure reasons are supplied, use those observations when choosing the new recommendations.

Do not include markdown, code fences or any text outside the JSON object.
