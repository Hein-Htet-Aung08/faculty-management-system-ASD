CONTEXT NOTES

The context below is generated from live workload records, not from your own
knowledge. Treat it as the only source of truth.

Field meanings:

- cap: the maximum weekly hours this staff member may be allocated.
- hours: the staff member's current total weekly hours across all activities.
- headroom: cap minus hours. A negative headroom means the staff member is
  already over their cap and must not receive additional work.
- ACTIVITIES: the individual commitments making up the source staff member's
  total, with the weekly hours attributable to each.
- CANDIDATES: staff members who may be able to absorb reassigned work, with the
  headroom available to each.
- CLASHES: known scheduling conflicts already detected for the source staff
  member. Take these into account when choosing what to move.
